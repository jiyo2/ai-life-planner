Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how AI Life Planner may collect, use and protect information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Travel destination
  • Travel dates
  • Number of travelers
  • Travel budget
  • Travel interests
  • Travel preferences and notes

We may also receive technical information such as browser type, device information and basic website usage information when necessary to operate and secure the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information provided to AI Life Planner may be used to:

  • Create personalized travel plans
  • Improve the travel planning experience
  • Provide accommodation and restaurant information
  • Provide transportation and activity suggestions
  • Maintain and secure the website
  • Process payments when paid services are used
  • Respond to support requests

3. AI Processing

Travel information submitted to the planner may be processed by artificial intelligence systems in order to generate travel plans and recommendations.

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive personal information into travel planning fields.

4. Payment Information

If you purchase a paid service, payment processing may be provided by a third-party payment processor.

AI Life Planner does not need to directly store your complete payment card information when payment processing is handled by the payment provider.

5. Third-Party Services

AI Life Planner may use third-party services to operate features of the website, including hosting, artificial intelligence, payment processing, mapping or travel-related information.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies when required for functionality, security, preferences or service operation.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the service. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct or request deletion of certain information.

10. Children's Privacy

AI Life Planner is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

11. Changes to This Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Notice or how information is handled, please contact us through the contact information provided on the AI Life Planner website.
